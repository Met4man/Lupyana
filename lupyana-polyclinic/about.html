About Lupyana Polyclinic

About the clinic

Lupyana Polyclinic is a 24-hour health centre in Vwawa, Mbozi, Songwe. We provide outpatient and inpatient care, surgical services, X-ray and laboratory diagnostics, and specialist care for mothers, children and eyes.

Our background

Serving Vwawa and Mbozi

Lupyana Polyclinic serves families in Vwawa, Mbozi and the wider Songwe region from its premises on Florida Street.

Our mission

What we work towards

To provide accessible, safe and respectful health care to the people of Vwawa, Mbozi and Songwe at any hour.

Our values

What guides the clinic

Care for every patient with dignity and respect.

Key facts

Key facts about the clinic

Open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Services include outpatient (OPD) and inpatient (IPD) care, surgery, X-ray, laboratory tests, dental, eye, maternal, child growth and gynaecology care.

Details are confirmed by the clinic. Please call or WhatsApp if you need to check anything.
